About Urban Track

The trust layer of urban development in Africa: certified expertise, verified impact, trusted by institutions.

In urban development, hiring and partnership decisions rest on CVs nobody can verify. Urban Track replaces self-declared claims with a simple mechanism borrowed from science: cross-confirmation. A company publishes the project it carried out; each named expert personally confirms their contribution. Only double-confirmed experience earns the certified badge: like co-authors on ResearchGate.

Experts keep a permanent professional ID (OX-XXXXXX), a public profile listing nothing but certified experience, and export-ready CVs in the formats donors expect: Harvard-MIT academic, AFD, World Bank. Companies build teams on verified track records. Donors and national agencies anchor the trust layer.

No manual validation

Certification is automatic once both parties confirm. Humans only arbitrate disputes.

Privacy first

Projects can be published privately while still certifying contributions.

Free for experts

Individual experts never pay. Companies fund the platform through job postings.
